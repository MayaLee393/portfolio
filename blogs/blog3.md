 ![Hearthstone Battlegrounds](/portfolio/blogs/images/blog3-cover.jpg)

Recently, I started playing Hearthstone Battlegrounds again. I wanted something fun to do 
instead of scrolling through social media. Battlegrounds is a game mode in Hearthstone 
where the players get a choice of heroes and each has a special power. 

The player has a pouch of gold, which increases by one each round, that they use 
to buy minions from the tavern, upgrade tiers it to get a better shop selection, or reroll to get 
different minions of the same tier. You can only have seven minions on the board and 
seven cards in your hand, so it is important to choose wisely. Some minions synergize really well 
with each other, but some can sabotage your own board if used incorrectly. 

Minions are categorized in to several groups called tribes: 
Mechs, Dragons, Murlocs, Demons, Quilboar, Undead, Beasts, Elementals, Naga 
and no types, with each having their own special resonance.
Generally, just getting minions of the same tribe is okay since they work well together.

There are many other elements to this game including spells and trinkets, and their mechanics 
but I don't think I'm skilled enough to explain how to pick or use them well, so I'll 
just go over a couple of my recent games that I thought was interesting.

## Elemental End of Turn Build
 ![Elemental End of Turn Build](/portfolio/blogs/images/blog3-hs-1.PNG)
 
 In this game, I started to pivot towards playing an Elemental build after getting a trinket(Felemental one) that boosts my stats 
 in the tavern. Elementals generally focus on either having high tavern stats or high elemental generation. 
 After gaining tempo by boosting and constantly switching out my elementals with ones from the tavern, I 
 got my next trinket(Drakkari's Portrait) and decided to switch to an end of turn build.

 The key components in my end of turn build was:
 - Elemental Drakkari: triggers end of turn effects twice and when golden, triggers end of turn effects three times
  
 - Bountiful Bedrock: get a random elemental at the end of every two turns, which triples with my Drakkari. 
   Also, since Drakkari is seen as an elemental, I also have a chance to get more of him, which is why I was able to get two 
   golden ones. (edit: I showed my friend who was playing longer than me, and he said this card is only mid game, and I should've taken this out a long time ago)
 - Unleashed Mana Surge: gives 3 of the elementals on my board +1/+1 for every elemental I play. This works well 
   after I get all the elementals from the Bedrock. Also, since my Mana Surge is golden, it happens twice.
 - Lokholar: gives my minions +3/+2 at the end of every turn (happens twice since it's golden), and this also works 
   well with my Drakkari.
 - Fauna Whisperer: this is the only non-elemental minion in my deck. It is a Naga that plays Natural Blessing on the card to it's
   left at the end of every turn. Natural Blessing gives all minions of the same type +3/+3, so all of my elementals will get stats. (edit: my friend also said this card wasn't good because the stats were weak, and that there are better cards I could have used instead)

## Amalgam and Mishmash Quilboar Build
 ![Amalgam and Mishmash Quilboar Build](/portfolio/blogs/images/blog3-hs-2.PNG)
 
 In this game, I made a lot of silly mistakes, but it ended up being a fun play. Somehow, I got third even though I wasn't too sure what I was doing.
 - Mistake 1: I originally was playing an undead build, and got a really good tier card for it. I got a buddy that transformed the minion next to it into one of a higher tier. I forgot to move it away from an important undead minion that I wanted to keep, so I ended up losing it. Fortunately, it transformed into a good Quilboar, so I decided to transition and play for it.
 
 - Mistake 2: I accidentally selected the Mishmash trinket. With that trinket, you get an Amalgam, which has all types, and a Mishmash (also all types) which gains stats when the Amalgam gains stats. I realized that this works well with Quilboars since every time I played a blood gem (spell that gives stats) on the Amalgam, the Mishmash would also get it's stats. 
 - Mistake 3: I wanted to try and triple the Mishmash since I was curious of what it was. I attempted to reborn it and use an undead to destroy it and keep the reborn copy on my board, but I forgot to leave space for the minion to get reborn. 

 From playing Battlegrounds, not only was I able to learn about tradeoffs, space and time management, 
 and strategizing based on patterns, which are important skills to master, I was able to have fun doing it. 
 
 Although there is a bit of a learning curve (trust me, I am still in the lower levels compared to others), 
 I think that taking the time to learn how to play was worth it. Just earlier, I pulled up 
 this [website](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/battlegrounds) with descriptions of all the battleground cards, so I know what to look out for.
