Recently, I started playing Hearthstone Battlegrounds again. I wanted something fun to do 
instead of scrolling through social media. Battlegrounds is a game mode in Hearthstone 
where the players get a choice of heroes and each has a special power. 
The player has a pouch of gold, which increases by one each round, that they use 
to buy minions from the tavern, upgrade tiers it to get a better shop selection, or reroll to get 
different minions of the same tier. You can only have seven minions on the board and 
seven cards in your hand, so it is important to choose wisely. Some minions synergize really well 
with each other, but some can sabotage your own board if used incorrectly. 
Minions are categorized in to several groups called tribes: 
Mechs, Dragons, Murlocs, Demons, Quilboar, Undead, Beasts, Elementals, Naga 
and no types, with each having their own special resonance.
Generally, just getting minions of the same tribe is okay since they work well together.
There are many other elements to this game including spells and trinkets, and their mechanics 
but I don't think I'm skilled enough to explain how to pick or use them well, so I'll 
just go over a few of my recent games that I thought was interesting.

## Elemental End of Turn Build
 ![Elemental End of Turn Build](/portfolio/blogs/images/blog3-hs-1.png)
 In this game, I started to pivot towards playing an Elemental build after getting a trinket that boosts my stats 
 in the tavern. Elementals generally focus on either having high tavern stats or high elemental generation. 

 From playing Battlegrounds, not only was I able to learn about tradeoffs, space and time management, 
 and strategizing based on patterns, which are core life skills to have, I was able to have fun doing it. 
 Although there is a bit of a learning curve (trust me, I am still in the lower levels compared to others), 
 I think that taking the time to learn how to play was worth it. Just earlier, I pulled up 
 this [website](https://hearthstone.blizzard.com/en-us/battlegrounds) with descriptions of all the battleground cards, so I know what to look out for.
