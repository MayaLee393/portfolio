## Houston Rodeo: Journey
I went back to Houston straight after my Software Engineering class, so that I could make it in time for the Journey concert. My relatives from Virginia also came to Houston just to see this concert. We were all big fans of Journey and would always have at least one Journey song played during karaoke.

After waiting for what felt like forever, it was finally time for the concert. We bought one $50 t-shirt and made our way to our seats, excited for the show. I wasn't familiar with the first two songs they performed, but I appreciated the firework and lightshow. The third song they performed was *Don't Stop Believing*&mdash;it's a classic song that everyone knew. However, halfway through the song, the lights and sound suddenly cut off. At first, we thought it was part of the act and the audience sung to finish the verse. Once the verse was over and the lights were still off, we realized that something was wrong. Here's a link to the [video](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1beXV-psDyPPXMg8ywbvYsiTP1_K98YN7/view?usp=drive_link). We stopped belieiving.

After waiting for 15 minutes, the technician finally came out and told us there was an electrical fire, and that we should have a good night. We did our walk of disappointment down the ramps of the NRG stadium, and sulked all the way to the car. What a way to start my spring break..
