## Longhorn Run
Today, I ran my first 5k in a long time, and I can proudly say I made it through. 
I've never been an atheletic person, so I didn't train much for this run. I did it purely on a 
whim, and dragged my friends along with me.

I started off running with my friends Edwin and Alana, but very quickly into the run, 
I got left behind. Our goal was to keep up with the 11 minute mile pacer, which I was 
a lot harder than I expected it to be. I would catch up to her for a few seconds before 
slowing down enough to lose her again. 

Luckily, I found one of my other friends, Nikki. We ran about the same pace and had a silent agreement 
that we were going to walk up every hill rather than run. I'm really glad I ran into her because we held 
each other accountable. Every five minutes, we'd turn to each other and say, "Let's run to that light post 
and then stop," or vise versa. 

Finally after around 37 minutes, we made it to the end. That's around a 12 minute mile, which 
isn't bad by my standards, but everyone else could me slow. This summer, I'll start running more 
and maybe get that time cut down so it's closer to 10.
